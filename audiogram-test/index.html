Check your hearing at home

  • Takes about 5 minutes, in a quiet room
  • Tests each pitch from low to high, in each ear
  • Free, and your result is shown on a chart

Headphones test each ear separately and are more accurate. Speakers work too.

This is a screening for a rough picture, not a medical diagnosis. For an accurate audiogram you can use with TellyHear, have a professional hearing test.

A few quick questions

This helps Lachlan tailor your results and check nothing needs a doctor first. It takes a few seconds, and your answers stay private.

Do you turn the TV or phone up louder than others prefer?
Is it hard to follow conversation in a noisy place?
Do people often seem to mumble, or you ask them to repeat?
Do you get ringing in your ears (tinnitus)?
Have you worn hearing aids before?
Any recent ear pain, discharge, or a sudden drop in hearing?

Those symptoms are worth a quick check with a GP or audiologist before an online screening. You can still continue below.
