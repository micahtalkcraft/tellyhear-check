  1. Do you turn the TV up louder than others prefer?

    One of the most common early signs.

  2. Is it hard to follow conversation in noisy places?

    Cafes, restaurants or busy family dinners.

  3. Do you often ask people to repeat themselves?

    In person or on the phone.

  4. Do you get ringing in your ears?

    Buzzing or humming counts too.

  5. Have you worn hearing aids before?

    Now or at any time in the past.

    Have you had any recent ear pain, discharge, or sudden hearing loss?

    These can need a professional check before an online test.

      How to read your audiogram

        What decibels (dB) mean

        Decibels measure how loud a sound is. On a hearing test, 0 dB is the faintest sound a healthy young ear can hear.

        Your number is how loud a sound had to be before you heard it, so a lower number is better. The scale is not even: every extra 10 dB sounds about twice as loud.
