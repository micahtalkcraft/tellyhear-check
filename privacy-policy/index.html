    1. About TellyHear

    TellyHear is operated by Tellyhear By Lachlan Smith Pty Ltd (“TellyHear”, “we”, “us” or “our”), an Australian private company based in Victoria, Australia.

    ABN: 59 700 760 185
    ACN: 700 760 185
    Website: tellyhear.com.au
    Email: admin@tellyhear.com.au
    Location: Moonee Ponds VIC 3039, Australia

    TellyHear provides hearing aids and associated hearing-related services to adults in Australia.

    Our services may include the online sale of hearing aids and accessories, hearing aid programming and configuration by an audiologist, remote hearing support, telehealth or online consultations, follow-up adjustments, warranty and repair assistance and other hearing-related services.

    We recognise that some of the information we handle is health information and therefore requires a higher level of privacy protection.

    We handle personal information in accordance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth), the Australian Privacy Principles (“APPs”) and, where applicable, the Health Records Act 2001 (Vic) and Victorian Health Privacy Principles.

    2. Scope of this Privacy Policy

    This Privacy Policy explains how TellyHear collects, holds, uses, discloses and protects personal information.

    It applies when you:

    • visit tellyhear.com.au
    • browse or purchase our products
    • create an account
    • submit an enquiry
    • provide information about your hearing
    • upload an audiogram, photograph or other document
    • communicate with us
    • receive hearing-related or audiology services
    • participate in a remote consultation
    • request support, adjustments, repairs or warranty assistance
    • otherwise interact with TellyHear.

    Additional privacy notices or consent statements may be presented at the point where particular information is collected.

    Those notices should be read together with this Privacy Policy.

    3. Adults only

    TellyHear’s products and hearing services are intended for persons aged 18 years and over.

    We do not knowingly provide our hearing services to, or intentionally collect health information directly from, persons under 18.

    If we become aware that we have inadvertently collected personal or health information relating to a person under 18 in circumstances where we should not have done so, we will take reasonable steps to deal with that information appropriately and in accordance with applicable law.

    • previous or current hearing aids

    • photographs of your ears, head or surrounding areas where relevant to hearing aid fitting or another service
    • documents uploaded to our website
    • information relevant to hearing aid suitability, fitting, programming, adjustments or support
    • any other health information you choose to provide to us.

    We may use artificial intelligence and third-party technology providers to extract information from audiograms, analyse hearing test results, process ear photographs and assist with the preparation of hearing assessment reports. These services may involve overseas processing of health information.

    We generally collect sensitive information where:

    • you have consented to its collection and the information is reasonably necessary for our functions or activities
    • collection is reasonably necessary to provide an appropriate health service and is otherwise permitted by law
    • collection is required or authorised by law
    • another exception under applicable privacy legislation applies.

    We may seek express consent through a checkbox, online consent process, written form, verbal consent or another appropriate method.

    If you do not provide health information that is reasonably required to provide a particular service, we may be unable to properly program or configure your hearing aids or provide the relevant hearing-related service.

    TellyHear uses artificial intelligence (AI) and automated communication technologies to assist with customer enquiries, appointment scheduling, follow-up communications, administrative tasks and the provision of our services.

    When you communicate with TellyHear by telephone, you may interact with an AI-powered voice assistant or a member of our team. With your consent, telephone conversations may be recorded, transcribed and processed using AI technologies.

    With your consent, telephone calls, video consultations and online fitting appointments may be recorded or transcribed. We may use third-party artificial intelligence services to process these recordings and transcripts, generate summaries, prepare clinical and administrative notes, and maintain accurate customer records.

    These services may involve processing personal and health information outside Australia. You may decline recording, transcription or AI-assisted note preparation and request an alternative arrangement.

    Information collected through these interactions may include:

    • your name and contact details
    • audio recordings and written transcripts
    • information about your hearing, relevant medical history and hearing aid requirements
    • information relating to appointments, enquiries and purchases
    • AI-generated summaries and notes
    • other information you voluntarily provide during the conversation.

    We may use this information to:

    • respond to enquiries and provide customer support
    • maintain accurate customer and clinical records
    • assist our audiologist and staff in providing appropriate services
    • manage appointments and follow-up communications
    • understand your hearing-related requirements
    • monitor service quality and resolve complaints
    • support our administrative and operational processes.

    We may engage third-party technology providers to facilitate telephone communications, speech recognition, transcription, speech synthesis, AI processing, customer relationship management and secure data storage.

    These providers may process, store or access personal and health information outside Australia, as described in Section 23 of this Privacy Policy.

    We take reasonable steps to protect personal and sensitive information processed through these technologies and to ensure that our use of third-party providers complies with applicable privacy obligations.

    Access to recordings, transcripts and AI-generated notes is restricted to authorised personnel and service providers who reasonably require access for relevant purposes.

    AI-generated information may be reviewed by authorised members of our team. Our AI voice assistants are not a substitute for professional audiological assessment, diagnosis or clinical advice.

    You may decline or withdraw consent to call recording and AI-assisted communication and request an alternative method of communication.

    Recordings, transcripts and associated information will be retained in accordance with Sections 26 and 27 of this Privacy Policy.

    You may request access to or correction of your personal information, or request its deletion, subject to applicable legal, regulatory and clinical record-keeping requirements, by contacting us using the details provided in Section 37.
