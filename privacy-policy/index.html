1. About TellyHear

TellyHear is operated by Tellyhear By Lachlan Smith Pty Ltd (“TellyHear”, “we”, “us” or “our”), an Australian private company based in Victoria, Australia.

ABN: 59 700 760 185

TellyHear provides hearing aids and associated hearing-related services to adults in Australia.

Our services may include the online sale of hearing aids and accessories, hearing aid programming and configuration by an audiologist, remote hearing support, telehealth or online consultations, follow-up adjustments, warranty and repair assistance and other hearing-related services.

We recognise that some of the information we handle is health information and therefore requires a higher level of privacy protection.

We handle personal information in accordance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth), the Australian Privacy Principles (“APPs”) and, where applicable, the Health Records Act 2001 (Vic) and Victorian Health Privacy Principles.

2. Scope of this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how TellyHear collects, holds, uses, discloses and protects personal information.

It applies when you:

  • visit tellyhear.com.au
  • browse or purchase our products
  • create an account
  • submit an enquiry
  • provide information about your hearing
  • upload an audiogram, photograph or other document
  • communicate with us
  • receive hearing-related or audiology services
  • participate in a remote consultation
  • request support, adjustments, repairs or warranty assistance
  • otherwise interact with TellyHear.

Additional privacy notices or consent statements may be presented at the point where particular information is collected.

Those notices should be read together with this Privacy Policy.

3. Adults only

TellyHear’s products and hearing services are intended for persons aged 18 years and over.

We do not knowingly provide our hearing services to, or intentionally collect health information directly from, persons under 18.

If we become aware that we have inadvertently collected personal or health information relating to a person under 18 in circumstances where we should not have done so, we will take reasonable steps to deal with that information appropriately and in accordance with applicable law.

5. Health and sensitive information

Health information is sensitive information under Australian privacy law.

Because TellyHear provides hearing-related services, we may collect a broad range of health information where reasonably necessary to provide those services.

This may include:

  • audiograms

  • information about hearing loss
  • hearing difficulties and symptoms

  • previous or current hearing aids
  • hearing aid settings
  • programming and fitting information
  • hearing aid usage information
  • clinical notes
  • consultation notes
  • information provided during telephone, video or online consultations
  • information supplied by an audiologist or healthcare professional
  • referral information
  • relevant medications or medical conditions where disclosed to us
  • photographs of your ears, head or surrounding areas where relevant to hearing aid fitting or another service
  • documents uploaded to our website
  • information relevant to hearing aid suitability, fitting, programming, adjustments or support
  • any other health information you choose to provide to us.

We generally collect sensitive information where:

  • you have consented to its collection and the information is reasonably necessary for our functions or activities
  • collection is reasonably necessary to provide an appropriate health service and is otherwise permitted by law
  • collection is required or authorised by law
  • another exception under applicable privacy legislation applies.

We may seek express consent through a checkbox, online consent process, written form, verbal consent or another appropriate method.

If you do not provide health information that is reasonably required to provide a particular service, we may be unable to properly program or configure your hearing aids or provide the relevant hearing-related service.
