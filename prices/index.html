More questions? Answered.

Are your prices per hearing aid or per pair?

Per hearing aid. A pair is double the price shown, and a recent hearing test takes $500 off a pair.

Are there any extra costs?

No. Fitting, follow-up adjustments, delivery and the 3-year manufacturer warranty are all in the price. The only other cost is a hearing test, if you don't have a recent one. We'll help you arrange it.

Will someone pressure me to buy?

No. Your quote is emailed to you, so you can read it in your own time and talk it over with your family. Lachlan's team may call to answer your questions, and you're under no obligation.

Do I need a hearing test?

Lachlan needs a recent hearing test (an audiogram) to program your hearing aids. Already have one? Send it in and save $500 on a pair. Don't have one? We'll help you arrange it.

How does the $500 discount work?

Send a recent audiogram and a photo of each ear through our secure form. Once it is approved, you get $500 off a pair of hearing aids.

I'm not good with technology. Is that a problem?

Not at all. Your hearing aids arrive already set to your hearing test, and Lachlan talks you through everything on a video call. A family member is welcome to join. You can also see him in person in Moonee Ponds.

Can I claim on my private health insurance?

Many extras policies include a hearing aid benefit. It depends on your fund, policy and claim history, so check with your fund. We'll help you claim.

I have a pension card. Can I get hearing aids for free?

You may. The Government's Hearing Services Program covers people with a Pensioner Concession Card or a DVA Gold Card (a Commonwealth Seniors Health Card doesn't qualify). It includes fully subsidised hearing aids, or you can put the subsidy towards a dearer model and pay the difference. TellyHear is a program provider, and Lachlan's team will check what applies to you.

Can I pay it off over time?

Yes. You can spread the cost with Afterpay, Zip or Klarna when you order.

What if they don't suit me?

Every order includes a 45-day money-back guarantee and a 3-year manufacturer warranty.

How long does delivery take?

Shipping is free Australia-wide. Your hearing aids are programmed to your hearing test first, then sent to you by tracked delivery.
