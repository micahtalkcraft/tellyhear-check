Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers before you request your quote.

Is the quote free?

Yes. Requesting a quote is free and does not commit you to anything.

Do I need an audiogram?

You can browse, compare prices and ask questions without a hearing test. For a proper recommendation and fitting though, Lachlan needs real hearing information rather than guessing what your ears need. A standard audiogram from a qualified hearing professional is the best information to provide.

Will someone call me?

The team may contact you by phone or email using the details you provide. They will review your enquiry and help identify suitable options before you receive pricing and recommendations.

Can you fit hearing aids remotely?

For many suitable customers, yes. Hearing aids can be programmed around your hearing information and then fine-tuned after you have used them in real situations such as conversations, television, restaurants and phone calls. Remote fitting is not appropriate in every case, so Lachlan may recommend another approach if your hearing or ear health needs it.

Is there any obligation to buy?

No. Requesting a quote is free and does not commit you to anything.

How quickly will I hear back?

The team reviews each enquiry and will contact you by phone or email using the details you provide. If you would rather call, you can phone TellyHear on 0485 058 588, Monday to Sunday, 8am to 7pm AEDT.
