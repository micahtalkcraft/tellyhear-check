Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers before you request your quote.

Why do your hearing aids cost less than many clinics?

We sell online and fit remotely, so we avoid high rent and staffing costs that many clinics fold into their quotes. These are genuine medical-grade hearing aids from eight manufacturers, not cheap amplifiers. Our prices start from $1,690 per hearing aid. The price already includes programming to your hearing test, fitting, follow-up adjustments, the 45-day money-back trial, the 3-year warranty and free shipping.

Will I be left on my own after I buy?

No. You deal with the same audiologist, Lachlan Smith, every time. He is an Australian Senior Clinical Audiologist with more than 15 years’ experience. He handles programming, fitting, and all adjustments. Most people need two or three adjustments over the first few weeks, with unlimited support at no extra cost for as long as you wear them. You can call, email or WhatsApp us 24/7 on 0477 965 027. James Anderson wrote in a Google review: “Back up service can’t be beat, and nor can the price.”

I’m not good with technology. Will I manage?

You will have plenty of help. Lachlan programs your hearing aids to your hearing test before they ship, so they arrive already set for you. Your fitting is a one-to-one video call with Lachlan, and a family member is welcome to join. In-person appointments in Moonee Ponds, Melbourne, may be available. Most models are rechargeable, so no tiny batteries to fiddle with. If you get stuck, we walk you through it on a video call.

What if the hearing aids don’t suit me?

You are covered by a 45-day money-back trial. You can use the hearing aids in your everyday life and work through adjustments with Lachlan at no cost. If they still don’t suit you, return them within 45 days for a full refund, provided they are not damaged and meet our return terms. Device faults are covered by the 3-year manufacturer warranty.

I don’t have a hearing test. Can I still get a quote?

Yes. The quote is free and does not commit you to anything. You can still request a quote and speak with our team. We will explain how to arrange a hearing test with a qualified professional, which is needed for a proper fitting. We can help you find a local clinic.
