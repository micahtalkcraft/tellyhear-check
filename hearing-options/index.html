Can hearing aids really be fitted over a video call?

It's a fair question. Your hearing test gives Lachlan the clinical starting point. The surprising part is what happens once your hearing aids arrive.

Your new hearing aids can measure your hearing themselves, right in your ears.

  • Tested in your own ears

    On a video call, Lachlan plays test sounds through your new hearing aids and fine-tunes them to how you actually hear.

  • Set up where you'll wear them

    Every room sounds different, so Lachlan adjusts them while you're at home, not in a soundproof booth.

  • Help is a quick call away

    Most people need a few small tweaks in the first weeks. From home, each one is a short video call.

More questions? Answered.

Are your prices per hearing aid or per pair?

Per hearing aid. A pair is double the price shown, and a recent hearing test takes $500 off a pair.

Can I really buy hearing aids online?

Yes. Lachlan programs your hearing aids to your hearing test before they ship, then fits and fine-tunes them with you by video call. In-person appointments in Moonee Ponds are available too.

Do I need a hearing test?

Lachlan needs a recent hearing test (an audiogram) to program your hearing aids. Already have one? Send it in and save $500 on a pair. Don't have one? We'll help you arrange it.

How does the $500 discount work?

Send a recent audiogram and a photo of each ear through our secure form. Once it is approved, you get $500 off a pair of hearing aids.

Is professional fitting included?

Yes. Lachlan programs your hearing aids to your hearing test and fits them with you by video call, or in person in Moonee Ponds. It is included in the price.

Can my hearing aids be adjusted remotely?

Yes. Lachlan can fine-tune your hearing aids with you by video call after your fitting, so small changes do not mean a trip to a clinic.

Can I see Lachlan in person?

Yes. As well as looking after people online, Lachlan sees people face to face at his clinic in Moonee Ponds, Melbourne.

What if they don't suit me?

Every order includes a 45-day money-back guarantee and a 3-year manufacturer warranty.

How long does delivery take?

Shipping is free Australia-wide. Your hearing aids are programmed to your hearing test first, then sent to you by tracked delivery.

Can I claim on my private health insurance?

Many extras policies include a hearing aid benefit. It depends on your fund, policy and claim history, so check with your fund. We'll help you claim.

Can I pay it off over time?

Yes. You can spread the cost with Afterpay, Zip or Klarna when you order.

I'm a pensioner or veteran. Can I get help?

You may be eligible for subsidised hearing aids through the Australian Government Hearing Services Program or DVA. Mention it in your quote and Lachlan's team will check what applies to you.
