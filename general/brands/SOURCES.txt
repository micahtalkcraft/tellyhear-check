ARTICLE IMAGE SOURCES AND LICENCES
==================================

These files were selected for editorial use in a hearing-aid brand guide. They are sourced from Wikimedia Commons rather than scraped from manufacturer marketing pages, so their reuse terms are clear.

IMPORTANT
- Keep the required attribution somewhere reasonably accessible on the website or in its image credits page.
- CC BY-SA images require attribution and ShareAlike for adaptations.
- The downloaded images are otherwise kept at their sourced dimensions. resound.jpg was converted from CMYK to RGB JPEG for normal web-browser compatibility.
- This file is a practical source record, not legal advice.
