    What is an audiogram?

    It's the chart a hearing test gives you. It shows the softest sound each ear can hear, at each pitch.

    • Left to right runs from low pitches to high pitches.
    • Top to bottom runs from soft to loud. The lower a mark sits, the louder that pitch has to be before you hear it.
    • X is your left ear, O is your right.
    • The shaded band is where everyday speech sits. Marks below it mean parts of conversations get missed.

    Lachlan programs your hearing aids to it before they're posted, so they're set to your hearing the day they arrive.

    Why we ask for a photo of each ear

    Most hearing aids sit behind your ear with a thin wire, the receiver, running into your ear canal. Your photos show Lachlan the size and shape of each ear, so he can choose the right receiver length and dome size and your hearing aids fit comfortably from day one.

    He also checks for anything to sort out first, like a build-up of wax.

    • Your phone guides you: line your ear up with the outline and tap
    • Each photo is checked as you take it
    • Only Lachlan and his team ever see them

    How does the $500 work?

    You get $250 off each hearing aid, so $500 off a pair. Send us a recent audiogram, or take our free online hearing test, plus a photo of each ear. Lachlan checks everything and texts you within 24 hours with your own shop link, and the $500 comes off automatically at checkout.

    I don't have an audiogram. Can I still claim?

    Yes. Choose "No" when we ask, and you take our free online hearing test as part of your claim, at home with headphones or speakers. It counts for the $500, and your results are saved to your account.

    How recent does my audiogram need to be?

    From the last 2 years. Hearing changes gradually, so a recent test gives Lachlan an accurate picture of your hearing today. A photo of the printed page or a PDF are both fine.

    Why do you need a photo of each ear?

    Most hearing aids sit behind the ear with a thin wire, the receiver, running into the ear canal. Your photos help Lachlan choose the right receiver length and dome size so they fit comfortably, and spot anything to sort out first, like a build-up of wax.

    What if I can't read my audiogram?

    You never need to. Take a clear photo of it, or upload the PDF, and Lachlan reads it for you. He uses it to program your hearing aids before they are posted.

    How long do I have to use it?

    7 days from when Lachlan texts you. We send you a reminder before it runs out, and if you need more time, call us on 0477 965 027.

    Who sees my photos and results?

    Only Lachlan and his team. Your photos and hearing results are stored securely and are only used to check your claim and fit your hearing aids.

    Do I need an audiogram to buy hearing aids?

    No, you can buy without one. With an audiogram or our online test, your hearing aids arrive programmed to your hearing instead of at factory settings, and you get $500 off a pair.
