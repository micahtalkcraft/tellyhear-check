Contact questions, answered

The quickest ways to reach us, visit us or talk it through with Lachlan.

How can I get in touch with TellyHear?

You can call us on 0485 058 588, email admin@tellyhear.com.au, or fill in the contact form on this page. Support is available 24/7, and we will reply by email or phone. Our online store ships free Australia wide.

Can I visit your clinic in person?

Yes, our clinic is at 24 Pratt St, Moonee Ponds VIC 3039. We are open Monday to Friday 9am to 5pm and Saturday 9am to 12pm, closed Sunday. You can book an in-clinic fitting with Lachlan Smith.

Can I talk to an audiologist before buying?

Absolutely. Every fitting and adjustment is handled by Lachlan Smith, a qualified Australian audiologist with more than 15 years of clinical experience. You can speak with him over a video call from home or visit our Moonee Ponds clinic.

How do I get a free personalised quote?

Visit our Get a Quote page to request a free personalised quote. If you send a recent audiogram, you will receive $500 off a pair of hearing aids. We will guide you to the right device for your hearing needs.

What support do you offer after I purchase?

Your purchase includes ongoing support and unlimited adjustments from Lachlan Smith. You also get a 45-day money-back trial and a 3-year manufacturer warranty, so you can try your hearing aids with confidence.

Prefer to talk it through?
