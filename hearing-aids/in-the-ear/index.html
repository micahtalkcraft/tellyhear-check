What are in-the-ear hearing aids?

In-the-ear hearing aids pack all their components into a small shell that sits inside the bowl of your ear or deeper in the ear canal. Completely-in-canal models sit even further in, making them very hard to spot. They work by capturing sound right at the ear opening and delivering amplified sound directly into the ear.

The main appeal is discretion, with some styles being near-invisible when worn. TellyHear’s range uses ready-to-wear click sleeves, so you avoid the wait and cost of a custom earmould. Even with their small size, many of these devices now come with convenient rechargeable batteries.

They tend to suit mild to moderate hearing losses and people who want a very low-profile solution. Because they sit in the ear, they may need a little more care to keep clean and free of moisture. TellyHear programs each device to your audiogram and then posts it to you, with our audiologist available for remote adjustments as you settle in.

Our in-the-ear devices range from $1,690 to $4,300 per aid. Across 6 models, you can choose near-invisible ready-to-wear styles or rechargeable options that suit your budget and daily life, all backed by Lachlan's personalised care.

Do invisible hearing aids work well?

Yes, modern in-the-ear devices can be highly effective and nearly invisible. Our ready-to-wear designs use soft click sleeves for a comfortable fit without a custom mould, and they deliver clear, natural sound for mild to moderate losses. Discretion does not mean compromising on performance.

Do I need a custom mould for an in-the-ear hearing aid?

Not for our ready-to-wear in-the-ear models. They use flexible click sleeves that sit securely in the ear canal without the wait or expense of a custom mould. This approach speeds up delivery and keeps you comfortable while still providing a discreet, tailored fit.
