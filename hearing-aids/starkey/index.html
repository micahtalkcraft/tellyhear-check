Starkey hearing aids

Which Starkey model is right for you?

If your days are mostly quiet, the Omega AI level 16 provides clear, effortless hearing for everyday clarity at home. Level 20 suits regular social settings, helping you follow conversations in cafes or at family dinners. For active, demanding lifestyles, level 24 gives you advanced processing, and all Omega AI models include activity tracking and fall detection for added peace of mind.

You can choose the rechargeable RIC RT or the tiny mRIC R for a discreet fit. A RIC 312 version with replaceable size 312 batteries is also available if you prefer traditional battery power. If you need help choosing, send your audiogram to TellyHear. Lachlan will review it and recommend the right Starkey model, and you will also get $500 off a pair.
