The new Starkey Omega AI hearing aid is built on the all-new G3 Gen AI Neuro Processor, Starkey’s most intelligent and adaptive hearing solution yet. It is designed to deliver clearer conversations, smarter connectivity and advanced wellness features. For Starkey, every step forward pushes innovation to enable hearing aid wearers to hear better and live better lives.

Omega AI is powered by Starkey’s next-generation deep neural network technology, the DNN 360. This advanced technology intelligently detects speech, movement, and background noise from every direction, creating directionality pattern for a variety of listening situations to improve speech intelligibility in complex listening environments.

Omega AI also introduces powerful new features:

  • DNN feature that continuously scans the environment for clearest hearing and improved spatial awareness.
  • The rechargeable receiver-in-canal (RIC) models boast up to 51 hours of rechargeable battery life for dependable all-day performance.
  • Enhanced waterproof durability designed to withstand active lifestyles – up to 1 metre with rechargeable RIC styles only.
  • Edge Mode+ for instant on-demand speech enhancement in noisy situations.
  • Bluetooth LE Audio and Auracast readiness for smoother streaming and future-ready connectivity.
  • TeleHear AI, using generative AI to help fine-tune hearing settings remotely.
  • Integrated wellness tools like respiratory rate monitoring, fall detection, activity tracking, and Balanced Builder exercises through the My Starkey app.

With sleek modern styling, intelligent automation, and personalized hearing support, Omega AI represents Starkey’s most advanced hearing aid platform yet – built to help wearers hear better, connect easier, and live more confidently every day.

Shipping & support, answered

How delivery, warranty and our audiogram discount work on every order.

Do you ship hearing aids anywhere in Australia?

Yes. TellyHear ships Australia-wide, including regional and remote addresses, with free tracked delivery on every order. Most orders arrive within 7 days, and you get the tracking details by email so you can follow the parcel to your door. Because the fitting is done remotely over video, you do not need a clinic nearby to be looked after properly.

How long does it take to ship my hearing aids?

We guarantee your hearing aids are shipped within 7 days. That is a shipping timeframe rather than a guaranteed delivery date, since transit time after dispatch depends on the courier and where you are in Australia. Tracking is provided so you can follow your order.

How do I track an existing order?

Every order gets tracking details by email once it ships, usually within a few days of your fitting being prepared. If you cannot find that email or want an update, contact the team with your name and order number and we will check where the parcel is. Most orders arrive within 7 days of dispatch on free tracked shipping.

Do I get a warranty?

Every hearing aid comes with a 3-year manufacturer warranty, and that is on top of the 45-day return period where you can send them back for a full refund if they are not right. The warranty covers device faults. Contact the team and we will handle the manufacturer process for you rather than leaving you to chase it. Coverage is subject to the manufacturer’s warranty terms.

What happens if something goes wrong after I buy?

Support does not stop when the parcel arrives. Get in touch and we will work out whether it is the fitting, the settings, the connectivity or an actual device fault. Programming and comfort issues are fixed remotely with Lachlan at no cost. A genuine hardware fault goes through the 3-year manufacturer warranty, and within the first 45 days you can simply return the hearing aids for a full refund.

How do I get $500 off a pair of hearing aids?

Send us a recent audiogram, ideally from the last 12 months, and we take $500 off a pair. Lachlan programs your hearing aids to your audiogram before they ship, so the discount comes with devices that are already tuned to your hearing the day they arrive.
