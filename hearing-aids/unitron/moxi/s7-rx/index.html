Unitron Moxi Smile S-RX Hearing Aids

Unitron Moxi Smile S-RX combines dedicated AI sound processing, rechargeable convenience and universal Bluetooth connectivity in a discreet receiver-in-canal design. Created for challenging listening situations, it is designed to make conversations easier to follow when speech and background noise are coming from multiple directions.

AI-powered speech clarity in noise

At the heart of Moxi S-RX is Unitron’s DEEPSONIC™ Deep Neural Network chip and SoundSonic360 technology.

SoundSonic360 continuously analyses the surrounding soundscape, identifies speech and separates it from competing background noise in real time. Rather than simply reducing the overall volume, it is designed to preserve voices while managing distracting sounds, helping conversations remain clearer in busy restaurants, family gatherings and other noisy environments.

Adapts automatically to your surroundings

Unitron’s Integra OS identifies changes in the listening environment and automatically adjusts the hearing aid settings.

The S9-RX provides the most advanced automatic support, including specialised processing for conversations in crowds and inside the car. The S7-RX retains the core AI speech separation and noise-management technology, with fewer automatic listening programs.

Personalised control through the Remote Plus app

The Unitron Remote Plus app allows you to adjust volume and listening programs from a compatible iPhone or Android smartphone.

Two optional AI-powered programs, SpeechSonic and NoiseSonic, provide additional control in difficult environments. The AI Noise Filter lets you adjust the balance between speech clarity and surrounding noise according to your preferences. You can also share in-the-moment feedback with your hearing care professional through the app.

Universal Bluetooth connectivity

Moxi S-RX connects directly to compatible Bluetooth-enabled smartphones, tablets and other devices, including Apple and Android products.

You can stream phone calls, music, podcasts and video audio through the hearing aids, while hands-free calling allows you to speak without holding your phone. Its multi-device connectivity also makes it easier to move between commonly used Bluetooth devices.

Rechargeable everyday power

The built-in lithium-ion battery provides an expected operating time of up to 18 hours, giving most users dependable power throughout the day.

Actual battery performance depends on hearing loss, enabled features, streaming time, wireless accessory use, sound environments and battery age. An optional ChargerGo RX provides portable charging while travelling or away from a power outlet.

Comfortable and easy to maintain

The lightweight receiver-in-canal design sits discreetly behind the ear, with a thin wire connecting to the receiver inside the ear canal.

Optional EasyGuard™ domes help protect the receivers from wax buildup, supporting reliable performance and reducing routine maintenance. The hearing aids are also designed for comfortable, all-day wear.
